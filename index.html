Memory.bible

Hide God's Word in your heart, one verse at a time.

Memory.bible is a free app that helps you and your family memorise Bible verses through short, daily practice. It's a digital companion to physical verse-memory card decks — use it on its own, or alongside the cards.

How it works

  • Learn each new verse step by step, piece by piece.
  • Practise with flip cards, fill-in-the-blank, typing the verse, choosing the missing word and matching verses to references.
  • Spaced repetition brings back the verses that need the most work, so practice time goes where it counts.
  • Daily streaks and progress stats keep you motivated.
  • Family profiles with age-appropriate verse sets for children, youth and adults.
  • Six translations: KJV, BSB, ESV, NIV, NKJV and NASB.
  • Add any verse you want to memorise, and sync your progress across devices.
  • Install it on your phone's home screen, with daily reminders and offline use.

Open Memory.bible — it runs in your browser. JavaScript is required to use the app.
